PetroTwin Technical Documentation

PetroTwin is an enterprise supervisory digital twin for heavy-oil thermal recovery (Cyclic Steam Stimulation) and artificial lift (Sucker Rod Pumping). Built strictly as an advisory, air-gapped decision-support platform, it couples reservoir thermodynamics with surface kinematics to eliminate premature rod failures and optimize steam-to-oil ratios.

Advisory Decision-Support Scope
Under no circumstances does PetroTwin directly actuate downhole pumps, boiler valves, or variable frequency drives (VFDs). It adheres to ISA-95 Level 3.5 DMZ isolation and requires formal four-eye engineering sign-offs.

1. System Architecture & Purdue Isolation

The platform consists of a modern Next.js 16 HMI dashboard, a high-performance Python FastAPI backend, TimescaleDB for high-frequency time-series telemetry, Redis for transient simulation caching, and analytical physics/ML solvers.

4. Coupled Thermal-Mechanical Physics

In ultra-heavy crudes (e.g. 16° API, 2,600+ cP at 30°C), reservoir steam injection radically alters fluid drag downhole. PetroTwin models this cross-subsystem propagation:

  • Thermal Inflow Relaxation: Steam injection raises near-wellbore temperature to ~180°C, dropping viscosity to <100 cP.
  • Drag Rebound: Post-soak cooling causes fluid viscosity to rebound exponentially, creating severe hydrodynamic drag on the sucker rod string during downstroke.
  • Rod-Float Prevention: When downward fluid drag exceeds buoyant rod string weight, the rod floats, causing compressive buckle and parted rods. PetroTwin automatically computes the maximum safe SPM to keep the string in tension.

5. Gibbs Damped Wave Equation Diagnostics

Surface load and position signals are transformed into true downhole pump cards by solving the one-dimensional damped wave equation: 

d²u/dt² = a² * (d²u/dx²) - c * (du/dt)

Where:
  u(x,t) = rod displacement at depth x and time t
  a      = acoustic velocity of stress waves in steel (~16,100 ft/s)
  c      = empirical damping factor (viscous fluid drag coefficient)

The ML classification engine extracts normalized Fourier shape descriptors and downhole harmonic ratios, achieving 99.77% condition classification accuracy across 6 diagnostic states (Normal, Fluid Pound, Gas Lock, Rod Float, Tubing Leak, Anchored Tubing).

7. Section 65B Cryptographic Audit Ledger

Every operational change, setpoint modification, and approval is recorded in an immutable, append-only ledger in TimescaleDB. Records are hashed sequentially (SHA-256) to ensure legal admissibility under Section 65B of the Indian Evidence Act, preventing retroactive tampering.
